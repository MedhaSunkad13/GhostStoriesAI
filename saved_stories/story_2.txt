Theme: The mirror that shows tomorrow

The antique mirror in my grandfather’s study showed only tomorrow, perfectly clear images of my future - each glance pulling me closer to terror.  I initially dismissed it as a trick of the light, but the reflections quickly coalesced into ghostly images of my past failings.  A chilling whisper echoed from within as it turned into a clear voice, warning me of a darkness yet to be embraced.Panic seized me, as the mirrored visage of myself moved closer with each terrifying glimpse.Now, I only see the mirror when the house is empty - and the reflections watch, always watching.
