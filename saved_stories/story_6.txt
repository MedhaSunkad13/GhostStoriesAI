Theme: A school constructed on graveyard

The old Black Creek High School stood on a hill overlooking a perpetual grey expanse - it was rumored built on an ancient graveyard, steeped in shadowed histories.  Despite the attempts to scrub the walls clean, strange whispers echoed through the halls at dusk, always seeming to come from a vacant room above the library. One night, children swore they saw spectral figures wandering the corridors, replaced by flickering light and icy drafts, seemingly moving between the empty classrooms.  The groundskeeper, now a lonely old man, insisted the school housed a tormented spirit, forever searching for a lost student.  Panic gripped the student body as they realized the school wasn't empty - it was hungry for lost lives, slowly consuming the present.
