Theme: A ghost in a hospital

Rain lashed against the windows of St. Augustine’s Hospital, always just before midnight, when a chill replaced the warm air. An elderly nurse, Clara, frequently swore she heard a mournful weeping sound from the vacant patient beds. She began noticing shadowy forms flickering at the periphery, always turning toward the white hallway. One night, a translucent woman in a crimson dress pressed a cold hand against her cheek, whisper saying, “He doesn’t let go…” and then, silence settled, leaving only the dripping rain.
