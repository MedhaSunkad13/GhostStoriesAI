Theme: The Diary That Writes Back.

The old diary sat shrouded in dust, its pages strangely warm to the touch. Locals whispered of it—The Diary That Writes, a voice eternally trapped within its weathered script. Slowly, ghostly lines began to bloom across the brittle parchment detailing a tragic confession – a murder, repeated with chilling accuracy.  A spectral hand reached out, appearing in a looping, angry sentence, and a chill permeated the room. I tried to close the book, but its words seemed to follow me, a lamenting whisper demanding revenge.
