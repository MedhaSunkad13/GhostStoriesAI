Theme: The last train at 12:01 AM

The old train screeched to a halt at 12:01 AM, its lights a cold, unsettling blue in the velvet darkness. A chill permeated the carriages, even colder than the drafts biting at the windowpane.  Then, the faint shuffling of feet, followed by the whisper of a woman’s sigh, echoed from the shadowy carriage ahead. Figures began to coalesce in the dark - gaunt, elongated faces that vanished before a gaze could be caught.  It wasn’t there for long, but the train remained silent, and the air filled with the unspoken understanding that the passengers hadn't arrived – they just stayed.
