Theme: Room Number 13 in an abandoned hospital

The peeling wallpaper of Room 13 in the abandoned St. Michael's Hospital seemed to absorb all light, creating perpetual shadows. A chill clung like ice to the air as I entered, noticing a small, tarnished music box on the far side of the room. An unnerving melody, mournful and slow, began to drift in the silence, and a shadowy figure flickered just outside my peripheral vision. It raised a hand, a pale, sorrowful face, and beckoned me closer.Panic seized my soul as the music swelled into a deafening wail, erasing any hope of escape from the room’s chilling embrace.
